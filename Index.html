About Nessyglow

Nessyglow is a beauty brand created to help every woman feel confident, beautiful, and comfortable in her own skin.

Meet the CEO

Nessyglow is led by its CEO, a makeup artist who believes that good makeup brings brighter days. Every client is treated as an individual: skin tone, features, outfit and personality all shape the final look.

Our Mission

Our slogan says it best: "More than makeup, a more confident you." We want you to leave your appointment feeling like the best version of yourself, not like someone else.
